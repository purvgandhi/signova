What Signova does

From a webcam to five Indian languages.

21-point landmarks

How It Works

        
      

      

        
1. Optical Tracking: Real-time MediaPipe extraction mapping 21 spatial hand landmarks.

        
2. Temporal Classification: Hybrid RF-LSTM model over a 30-frame FIFO buffer with confidence gating (≥ 70%).

        
3. Sentence Synthesis: Gesture sequences reconstructed into fluent sentences using local SLM intelligence.

        
4. Regional Translation: Direct translation to Marathi, Hindi, Gujarati, Tamil, and Telugu with local speech synthesis.
